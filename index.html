A call pipeline that sounds studio clean

Every microphone stream passes through a processing chain before it reaches your partner — the same idea as AirTALK, tuned harder.

1. Clean capture

We request 48 kHz mono with browser-level echo cancellation, noise suppression and auto gain — so speaker echo and hiss never leave your device.

2. Noise gate

A high-pass filter removes rumble below 95 Hz, then a smart gate silences your mic the moment you stop talking. Fan noise, traffic and keyboard taps drop away.

3. Clarity & level

A compressor keeps your voice steady whether you whisper or shout, and a limiter prevents clipping. Both sides hear the same balanced level.

4. Direct WebRTC link

Audio travels peer-to-peer (with TURN fallback) so latency stays under ~150 ms. Adaptive bitrate + ICE restart keep the call alive on weak Wi-Fi.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is GOD'S WILL really free?

Yes. Voice calls, text chat, images and voice notes are all free. No premium wall, no signup, no credit card.

Do I need to create an account?

Never. You get a random nickname like Grace_4821 which you can change anytime. Nothing is stored about you except your nickname and anonymous usage counters.

How is the voice so clear with no background noise?

Your microphone is captured at 48 kHz with echo cancellation, then filtered (high-pass + noise gate) and levelled with a compressor before being sent peer-to-peer over WebRTC. Fans, traffic and keyboard noise get removed on your device, so your partner hears just your voice.

Can I chat while I'm on a call?

Yes — the call becomes a docked bar and the chat keeps working underneath. Send text, images and voice notes during the call, exactly like AirTALK.

What is the maximum image size?

20 MB per image by default. The owner can raise or lower this from the admin panel. Voice notes are also capped at 20 MB / 3 minutes.

Is my camera used?

No. There is no camera feature at all — the site never asks for or requests video permission.

What happens if my internet drops?

The call tries an automatic ICE restart first. If your connection really died, your partner sees a notice and you can rejoin the queue with one tap.

How many people can use the site at once?

The owner sets a capacity limit (slots) from the admin panel. When all slots are busy, new visitors see a friendly "try again in a few minutes" message instead of a broken page.

Can I talk to a specific friend instead of a random person?

Yes. Create a private invite room, copy the link and share it. Your friend joins straight into the same room — voice, text and media included.

How do I report someone?

Tap the 🚩 report button in the chat header. The admin console gets the report with the time and reason, and repeated offenders can be auto-banned.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The chat and call interface is fully responsive, and it works in Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox on both mobile and desktop.

Ready to talk to a stranger?

Thousands of people are online right now. Pick a mode and say hello.
